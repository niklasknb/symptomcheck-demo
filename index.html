Check your symptoms first

Read this. Then decide where to go.

A short, honest check can tell you whether you need the emergency room right now, a quick call to your doctor, or just some rest at home. It takes about two minutes — a few yes/no questions, nothing more.

Answer for yourself, and honestly: it's the only way this actually helps you get to the right place.

1. Quick check

A handful of targeted yes/no questions about your symptoms.

2. Clear reading

Red, Orange, Yellow, Green or Blue — the same scale hospitals use.

3. The right place

Ambulance, ER, or your local doctor — not the ER by default.
